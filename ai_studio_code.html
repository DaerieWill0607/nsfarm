NS Farm Vietnam is a Vietnam-based agricultural products supplier focused on connecting international buyers with suitable sources of Vietnamese agricultural products.

Our current product portfolio focuses on coconut and coconut-based products, with opportunities to expand into other agricultural commodities according to market demand.
